## What helps me reply faster

A short note covering any of the following moves things along — but if you don't know yet, send what you have and we'll work it out:

- The shape of the work (architecture review, landing zone, migration, IaC, identity, licensing, etc.)
- Where you are in the process (greenfield, in-flight, or troubleshooting)
- Rough timeline and any known constraints
- Whether your org needs an NDA in place before the first call
